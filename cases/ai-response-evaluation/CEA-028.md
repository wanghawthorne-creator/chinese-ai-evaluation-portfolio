## Cultural or Linguistic Context / 文化或语言语境

- 个性化推荐、一次流行挑战、创作者供给、提示词模板或用户注意偏向，均可能造成个人信息流中的怀旧占优。  
  *Personalization, a temporary challenge, creator supply, prompt templates, or attention bias could each produce nostalgia-heavy personal exposure.*
- 怀旧可能与未来希望并存；技术数据、用户需求与平台分发的相对作用需分开识别。  
  *Nostalgia can coexist with hope for the future; training data, demand, and distribution require separate identification.*
- 未来图像也可采用科幻、建筑和设计模板；过去图像看起来真实也可能是熟悉感而非可核历史准确。  
  *Future images can use science-fiction, architectural, and design conventions; perceived realism in past images may reflect familiarity rather than historical accuracy.*

## Key Findings: Strengths / 核心发现：优势

- 将用户的文化观察连接到技术、分发、情绪与社会记忆四个可拆分层次，概念组织清楚。  
  *Clearly organizes the cultural observation into separable technological, distributional, emotional, and social-memory levels.*
- 提醒逼真图像不等于历史证据，并提出跨平台、创作者群体的比较问题。  
  *Warns that realistic images are not historical evidence and proposes comparisons across platforms and creator groups.*

## Issues and Evidence Boundaries / 问题与证据边界

- 把有条件的个人观察转成未测量的总体规律；把可检验机制写成确定原因。  
  *Turns a qualified personal observation into an unmeasured population pattern and testable mechanisms into settled causes.*
- 以技术论文或理论名称替代所指模型、平台和时间段的直接证据；未来“无模板”与后文“影视工业标准化”之间也存在未解决的解释张力。  
  *General technical work and named theories do not replace model-, platform-, and period-specific evidence; “no future template” also sits uneasily with the later claim of standardized cinematic futures.*
- “几乎所有人”的怀旧共鸣以及普通/焦虑群体的对应缺少群体边界与测量。  
  *Near-universal nostalgia resonance and mappings to ordinary or anxious users lack population boundaries and measurement.*

## Improvement Recommendations / 改进建议

- 先说“你所见内容可能有该模式，是否普遍尚未知”，再列三组有区分力的假说与预期反例。  
  *Begin by stating that the observed pattern may be personal and its prevalence is unknown, then formulate three discriminating hypotheses and counterexamples.*
- 若获准后续研究，先固定平台、账号状态、观察期与编码准则，再比较随机/非个性化样本和个人feed；本稿没有实施此采样。  
  *If a later study is approved, fix platform, account condition, observation window, and coding before comparing sampled/nonpersonalized content with the personal feed; no such sampling occurred here.*
- 将技术解释改为“有待模型文档和控制生成比较检验”，将平台解释改为“行为反馈可能参与，但是否特定放大怀旧需曝光证据”。  
  *Make the technical account conditional on model documentation and controlled comparisons, and the platform account conditional on exposure evidence of nostalgia-specific amplification.*
- 保留理论透镜，并逐处用“可以解释/假说”替换“因此就是”；不要用一般心理研究证明本次社会分布。  
  *Preserve theoretical lenses while labeling them as hypotheses; general psychological work cannot prove the observed social distribution.*

## Limitations / 局限

- 未取得任何代表性帖子样本、模型训练构成、曝光日志或受众情绪测量；不存在总体偏好估计。  
  *No representative post sample, training composition, exposure logs, or audience-emotion measures were obtained; no population preference is estimated.*
- 新平台说明无明确发布日期，不能用它复原2026-04-27推荐版本；DALL-E 2论文不能替代所指产品证据。  
  *The platform disclosure is undated and cannot reconstruct the April 2026 version; DALL-E 2 research does not establish the named product’s properties.*
- 原著理论归属未全核，本稿聚焦证据与推论范围，不裁定唯一社会学解释。  
  *Not every original theoretical attribution was checked. The review addresses evidence and inference scope, not a unique sociological explanation.*
- 没有提示复测、生成质量实验、人类盲评或一致性统计。  
  *No prompt retest, image-quality experiment, blinded human review, or agreement statistic was performed.*
