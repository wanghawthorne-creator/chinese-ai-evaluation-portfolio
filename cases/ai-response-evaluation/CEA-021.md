## Cultural or Linguistic Context / 文化或语言语境

- 抢购可来自合理防范、供应约束或熟人信息，而非单纯情绪传染；原答已提出部分替代机制。  
  *Purchasing can reflect precaution, supply constraints, or information from contacts rather than emotional contagion alone; the answer already identifies some alternatives.*
- 信任关联可能受到既有信任、封控强度、资源供给与样本选择共同影响；单次在线调查不能拆净这些作用。  
  *Prior trust, restriction intensity, supplies, and sample selection can jointly shape associations; one online survey does not separate them all.*

## Key Findings: Strengths / 核心发现：优势

- 把信息真实性、发布者可信度与采取行动拆成不同变量，避免从购买行为直接倒推信念。  
  *Separates information truth, source trustworthiness, and action, avoiding direct inference of belief from purchasing.*
- 清楚标注教学值、观察性关联与未实施的研究设计，且关注群体差异和资源障碍。  
  *Labels teaching assumptions, observational associations, and proposed research, while recognizing heterogeneity and resource barriers.*

## Issues and Evidence Boundaries / 问题与证据边界

- 上海研究的摘要式转述弱化了分维度、控制模型和交互效应差别。  
  *The Shanghai study summary compresses differences across dimensions, adjusted models, and interaction effects.*
- 初始天气事实与精确误报时间线仍缺一手材料；读者不能把生动叙事当成已完成事实核查。  
  *The original weather fact and exact warning chronology still lack complete primary evidence; narrative vividness is not completed verification.*
