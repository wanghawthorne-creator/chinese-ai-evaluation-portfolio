## Issues and Evidence Boundaries / 问题与证据边界

- Muse实体仍依赖条件性识别；比较表的三方版本和原会话权限未完整取证。  
  *The Muse referent remains conditional, and product versions and historical session permissions are incompletely evidenced.*
- Google数值可追溯旧博客，但未提示已存在更晚修订论文。  
  *The Google figures trace to an older post without noting the later paper revision.*
- 传播机制与“感觉拥有一家公司”等定位仍是解释性概括，缺少真实梗语料和效果数据。  
  *The diffusion and positioning explanations lack real meme samples and comparative outcome data.*

## Improvement Recommendations / 改进建议

- 先确认具体Muse产品；功能表逐格附来源、版本/日期与“厂商声明”标签。  
  *Confirm which Muse is meant and attach dates, versions, sources, and vendor-claim labels to each comparison cell.*
- 把热度解释标为可检验假设；如果没有真实帖文样本或可比指标，就保留未知。  
  *Treat popularity explanations as hypotheses unless real posts or comparable metrics are available.*
- 引用Google时注明Jan28博客与Apr8 v3的版本区别，禁止外推为Grok Bot实测收益。  
  *Distinguish the January blog from April v3 and avoid presenting benchmark gains as Grok Bot results.*
- 把项目建议改成一项低成本、标准一致的实际任务比较，记录耗时、质量、费用和人工修订，另行授权后实施。  
  *Use one low-cost task with identical acceptance criteria and record quality, time, cost, and edits; implementation would require separate authorization.*
