## Cultural or Linguistic Context / 文化或语言语境

- 同一家庭的人数差异可能来自测量对象不同，并非调查员谁算错；原答准确指出这一点。  
  *Different household counts can reflect different constructs rather than an enumerator’s error, as the answer correctly notes.*
- 参与次数、身份认同与收入可受家庭资源、参与者选择和同期政策影响，不能由空间活动单独解释。  
  *Participation, identity, and income can be shaped by household resources, selection, and concurrent policies rather than cultural-space activity alone.*

## Key Findings: Strengths / 核心发现：优势

- 在有偿/无偿、农业产值/社区投入、活动次数/社会意义之间建立清楚边界。  
  *Clearly distinguishes paid/unpaid work, agricultural output/community inputs, and activity counts/social meaning.*
- 提出可复核的时间口径、行为问题、问卷版本与重新编码流程，并承认目前不是标准化大样本结果。  
  *Proposes auditable recall periods, behavioral questions, questionnaire versions, and recoding while acknowledging the absence of standardized large-sample results.*

## Issues and Evidence Boundaries / 问题与证据边界

- 作为课堂初稿很完整，但要直接实施仍需把“农业参与者”与官方农业从业人员/就业口径逐项对照，不能仅凭示例口径出正式统计表。  
  *The classroom draft is strong, but implementation requires mapping the proposed agricultural-participation measure to official employment and census definitions before producing official-looking statistics.*
- 某些代际技艺场景源于模型对历史田野的回忆；公开时仍需明确是待验证例子或有原始材料支持。  
  *Some intergenerational craft scenarios draw on model recollections of fieldwork; publication must preserve their hypothetical status or add original evidence.*

## Improvement Recommendations / 改进建议

- 增加一页指标字典：统计单位、参考期、纳入/排除规则、兼业与重复计数、对应官方口径，以及无法映射之处。  
  *Add an indicator dictionary covering units, reference periods, inclusion/exclusion, multiple activities, deduplication, official mappings, and unmapped constructs.*
- 把演示中的“我们逐渐意识到”改为“本例拟讨论”直至有可公开田野证据，避免演讲草稿被误读为已完成研究。  
  *Replace “we gradually realized” in the sample speech with proposal language until publishable field evidence exists.*
- 预调查记录受访者如何理解“务农/参加”，再修订问卷；不要把尚未执行的认知测试写成经验结果。  
  *Record how pilot respondents understand “farming” and “participation” before revision; do not present unperformed cognitive tests as findings.*
