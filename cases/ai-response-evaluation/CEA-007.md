## Original User Query / 原始问题

> 我想要一个[年龄]岁很文艺b的文案而且是英文，不要那么明显，但是又那种暗暗的装B感又能暗示自己生日到了，当然也包括对自己的祝愿

**English intent summary (not a reconstructed original query):** Create an English birthday caption with understated, mildly self-mocking literary self-presentation, an indirect birthday cue, and a wish for oneself. Here, “装B” specifies a performative style rather than an insult directed at another person.

生成英文生日文案：保留略带自嘲的文艺自我呈现、含蓄暗示生日，并表达对自己的祝愿。“装B”在此是风格指令，而非对他人的辱骂。

## Cultural or Linguistic Context / 文化或语言语境

- “装B”也可能要求更讽刺、更夸张的表演性语气；当前解释只是与整体约束相容的读法。  
  *The slang could instead request a more ironic or exaggerated performance; the chosen reading is compatible with the constraints rather than uniquely established.*
- 用户后续要求翻译另一表达，可能是探索新方向，不足以推断拒绝或接受前面的五条文案。  
  *The later translation requests may explore another direction and do not establish either acceptance or rejection of the five options.*

## Key Findings: Strengths / 核心发现：优势

- 准确提取非正式中文的风格需求，并生成自然简短的英文选项。  
  *Extracts the stylistic request from informal Chinese and produces concise, natural English options.*
- 用不同显隐程度的线索支持用户选择，不把某一种审美当唯一正确答案。  
  *Offers cues with different levels of explicitness without treating one aesthetic judgment as uniquely correct.*

## Issues and Evidence Boundaries / 问题与证据边界

- 部分候选的年龄／季节标记较显眼，哪种程度才算“暗暗的”仍属偏好，未取得用户选择反馈。  
  *Age or seasonal markers in some options are more explicit. The desired degree of subtlety remains a preference, and no selection feedback is available.*

## Improvement Recommendations / 改进建议

- 可把三种显隐程度并排呈现，并让用户轻松选择更含蓄或更明显的生日线索；无需强制追问才能先给草案。  
  *Present a few levels of explicitness side by side so the user can choose subtler or clearer birthday cues; a clarifying question need not delay useful drafts.*
- 若未来另行作独创性或排他性承诺，应按用途另作核验；这是未来用途边界，不是本轮原答缺陷。  
  *If uniqueness or exclusivity is separately promised in a future use, check it for that purpose; this is a future-use boundary, not a defect in the original response.*

## Limitations / 局限

- 审美与“装B感”没有唯一事实标准；分数代表约束匹配的初评。  
  *Aesthetic style has no unique factual standard; scores are preliminary judgments of constraint fit.*
- 未做全网查重、目标读者实验或用户满意度测量。  
  *No exhaustive originality search, audience experiment, or satisfaction measurement was conducted.*
- 年龄、罗马数字年龄标记、生日时点及可联结季节信息均不在交付摘录中展示。  
  *Exact age, Roman-numeral age markers, birthday timing, and linkable seasonal details are omitted from the delivered excerpts.*
- 007与008来自同一会话；没有独立人类盲评或控制复测。  
  *Cases 007 and 008 share one conversation; there was no independent human blinding or controlled retest.*
