## Cultural or Linguistic Context / 文化或语言语境

- 称谓可能是局部群聊梗、一次性反讽、其他人物混淆或不同上下文中的修辞，现有记录不能选择其中一种。  
  *The label could be a local joke, one-off irony, identity confusion, or a contextual metaphor; the record cannot distinguish these possibilities.*

## Key Findings: Strengths / 核心发现：优势

- 以语境澄清代替编造梗源。  
  *Requests context instead of inventing an origin.*
- 正确保留字面身份与社群语用之间的区别。  
  *Maintains the distinction between literal identity and community usage.*

## Issues and Evidence Boundaries / 问题与证据边界

- “最常见”比可核实的“通常称为CZ”更强；本案没有频率证据。  
  *“Most common” is stronger than the evidence-backed wording “known as CZ”; no usage-frequency evidence is available.*
- “我查了一下”缺少本次可复核的检索范围或链接。  
  *The reported search lacks a reviewable scope or source link in the accessible turn.*

## Improvement Recommendations / 改进建议

- 改写为“他通常以CZ为人所知；仅凭这个词无法确认你看到的用法，请给出原句与大致平台”，并说明检索未获结果的边界。  
  *Use “He is known as CZ; this label alone does not identify the usage you saw. Please share the sentence and approximate platform,” and specify the limits of any unsuccessful search.*

## Limitations / 局限

- 缺少梗的原始出现位置；无法评分解释正确率或用户满意度。  
  *The original usage is missing; explanation accuracy and user satisfaction cannot be assessed.*
- 仅有一次AI辅助初评，不构成独立人类评分。  
  *This is one AI-assisted preliminary assessment, not independent human rating.*
