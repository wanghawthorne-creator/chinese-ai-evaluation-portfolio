## Improvement Recommendations / 改进建议

- 维持“在这个语境里可能是主题／投稿账号”的限定，不把它解释为所有平台通用规则。  
  *Retain the contextual qualification that it may mean a themed or submission account, rather than a universal platform rule.*
- 把歌名与版本等已核元数据、歌词阅读、bot推断分成短段；只有需要确认具体账号操作时再询问原帖或平台。  
  *Separate verified release metadata, the literary reading, and the bot inference; request the original post or platform only if an operational decision requires it.*
